# Checkbox

Lets people pick any number of options from a list, or switch a single setting on. For one choice out of several, use a radio button. For a setting that applies straight away, use a toggle.

<Example
  name="checkbox/overview"
  caption="The parent shows a dash when only some stages are picked."
/>

```tsx
import { Checkbox } from '@/components/ui/selection-controls'
```

Pass `checked` and `onChange` to control it, or `defaultChecked` to let it keep its own state. `checked="indeterminate"` draws a dash and tells screen readers it's partly checked: use it on a parent whose children are only partly picked.

## Sizes

| Size | Box  | Radius | Border (unchecked) | Icon | Hover area       | Label                    |
| ---- | ---- | ------ | ------------------ | ---- | ---------------- | ------------------------ |
| `18` | 18px | 6px    | 1.4px              | 14px | 28px, 10px round | 14px (default)           |
| `15` | 15px | 5px    | 1.2px              | 12px | 21px, 8px round  | 13.8px                   |
| `14` | 14px | 5px    | 1px                | 12px | 20px, 8px round  | Not in Figma (uses 15px) |
| `11` | 11px | 3px    | 1px                | 10px | 17px, 5px round  | 12px                     |

`sm`, `md` and `lg` still work and mean `11`, `15` and `18`.

## Label and hover area

The label sits on the right by default, or on the left with `textPosition="left"`. With `textPosition="none"`, give it an `aria-label`. Labels are secondary grey, Regular weight.

`hoverable` (on by default) adds Figma's rounded square behind the box on hover: light grey when unchecked, light blue when checked. Turn it off where rows already highlight on hover, such as list items.

## States

<CheckboxStates />

Hover fades in over 70ms (Figma's ease in) and out over 50ms (ease out). Checking takes 10ms and unchecking 50ms. Disabled fades the whole checkbox, label included: to 30% unchecked and 40% checked.

**Figma oddities, copied as they are:**

- At 11px, the unchecked hover area uses the light blue fill, and an unchecked box with no label and no hover area has a 0.5px border.
- A few disabled variants differ: 18px unchecked with the label on the left fades to 40%; 11px checked with a label and hover area fades to 30%; 18px with a hover area and no label shows a 12px icon; 18px with a hover area and the label on the right uses a Medium label; 11px unchecked with a label and no hover area uses a 13px label.

## Usage

<DoDont>
  <Guideline kind="do">
    Use it for options people can combine: "Remote", "Hybrid", "On-site".
  </Guideline>
  <Guideline kind="dont">Use it for one choice out of several. Use a radio button.</Guideline>
  <Guideline kind="do">
    Make the label say what happens when it's checked: "Email me new applicants".
  </Guideline>
  <Guideline kind="dont">
    Write a negative label like "Don't send emails". It's hard to read when checked.
  </Guideline>
</DoDont>

## Accessibility

- **Keyboard:** Tab to reach it, Space to check it. The real `<input type="checkbox">` is kept in the page, hidden visually.
- **Focus:** when you Tab to it, it shows its hover fill plus a 1px blue ring (`bg/selection-controls/selected`, the blue the buttons use for focus). It only appears for keyboard focus, not on click.
- **Label:** the whole row, label included, is clickable. Without a visible label, pass `aria-label`.
- **Indeterminate:** sets `aria-checked="mixed"`.
