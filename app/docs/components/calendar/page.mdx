# Calendar

A month of days to pick one from. It opens from the [date input](/docs/components/date-input), and can be used on its own.

<Example name="calendar/overview" />

```tsx
import { Calendar } from '@/components/ui/input'
```

## Layout

| Variant            | Width | Columns apart |
| ------------------ | ----- | ------------- |
| Loose, wrapped     | 264px | 8px           |
| Loose, not wrapped | 256px | 8px           |
| Tight, wrapped     | 228px | 2px           |
| Tight, not wrapped | 220px | 2px           |

`spacing` picks loose or tight; `wrapped={false}` drops the card, for a calendar that sits inside another surface. Weeks start on Monday, and a month shows only the weeks it needs (4 to 6 rows), so the height changes from month to month.

### Year and month lists

Clicking the year or the month opens its list. The button itself doesn't change.

| Property  | Value                                                                                                              |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Card      | 🦄 Slots, light, 100 × 227px, 4px padding, 10px corners, 0.5px border, small shadow                                |
| Position  | 46px above the calendar's top edge, over the header                                                                |
| Left edge | year: 0. Month: 68px (loose, wrapped), 79px (loose, not wrapped), 50px (tight, wrapped), 61px (tight, not wrapped) |
| Years     | 14: nine years back to four ahead (2017 to 2030 in Figma), or from `min`'s year                                    |
| Months    | January to December                                                                                                |
| Scroll    | the year list opens at its end, the month list at the top                                                          |

Each entry is Figma's 🖱️ List Item (the 32px one, set to 24px):

| Property | Value                                                                                           |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Size     | full width (92px), 24px tall                                                                    |
| Padding  | 6px left, 8px right                                                                             |
| Corners  | 8px                                                                                             |
| Text     | Body S Regular, 13px on a 19.5px line, `text/secondary`                                         |
| Hover    | a 4% fill (`bg/transparent/light`); the text doesn't change. 100ms, ease in; back with ease out |
| Current  | not marked (Figma marks none)                                                                   |

Both lists scroll with Figma's scrollbar (🖱️ List Item/.Subcomponents/scrollbar), laid over the card's right edge:

| Property    | Rest                                                                  | Hover or dragging                                   |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Track       | 12px wide, full height, no fill                                       | `bg/transparent/light` (4% black; 6% white in dark) |
| Thumb       | 2px, round, `text/input/hover` (#b3b3b3; #4c4c4c in dark)             | 4px, `text/light` (#b3b3b3; #666 in dark)           |
| Thumb inset | 6px from the top and bottom, 4px from the right; it grows to the left | the same                                            |
| Motion      | back to rest over 50ms, ease in                                       | in over 100ms, ease in                              |

The thumb's length follows the content (at least 24px). The browser's own scrollbar is hidden; the wheel, keyboard and touch still scroll as usual, and the thumb can be dragged or the track clicked.

<Example name="calendar/min" />

With `min`, earlier days are disabled and the year list starts at `min`'s year.

**Figma oddities, copied as they are:**

- Hover comes in with ease out and leaves with ease in, the reverse of other components.
- Today has no hover.
- The year and month buttons stay at Rest while their list is open, and no item in the list is marked as the current one.
- The lists cover the header.
- The month list's left edge is different in every variant (68, 79, 50, 61px).
- The list entries are the 32px List Item squashed to 24px.
- A day at Rest has square corners; only the filled states are round, so it never shows.

**Not in Figma, kept in code:**

- `min`, which disables earlier days.
- Keyboard focus: a 1px blue ring round a day (the selection controls' ring), and on a list entry the hover fill plus the ring. Figma has no focus state.
- The keyboard support below.
- When the shown year or month is out of view, its list opens with it in the middle (Figma only shows this year and June).
- The lists show Figma's scrollbar; Figma's calendar lists only have the scroll hint.
- The list's scroll hint is the Slots fade. Figma's is a 28px fade with a 10px chevron in `text/light`, at the top once scrolled and at the bottom while there's more; it comes with the Slots audit.

## Accessibility

- It's a dialog named "Choose a date", with the days in a grid named by the month and year.
- Each day is a button named by its full date ("10 June 2026"); today is marked as the current date and the chosen day as selected.
- The year and month buttons say whether their list is open, and the lists are listboxes.

**Keyboard** (not in Figma):

| Key | Does |
| --- | --- |
| Tab | Reaches one day: the chosen day, else today, else the month's first day that can be picked |
| ← → | Previous or next day, into the next month when needed |
| ↑ ↓ | Same day of the previous or next week |
| Home, End | Monday or Sunday of that week |
| Page Up, Page Down | Same day of the previous or next month; with Shift, a year |
| Enter, Space | Picks the day |
| In a year or month list | ↑ ↓ Home End move, Enter picks, Escape closes the list and goes back to its button |

Days before `min` can't be reached with the arrows.
